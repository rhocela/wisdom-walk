# Bible Bridge: 365-Day Chronological Reading Plan

## **PLAN OVERVIEW**

**Total Coverage**: 1,189 chapters across 66 books  
**Duration**: 365 days  
**Reading Model**: Chronological (following biblical timeline, not book order)  
**Daily Target**: 25-35 minutes of Scripture reading (37-52 verses typically)

---

## **PART STRUCTURE (52 Parts)**

### **PART 1: In the Beginning** (Days 1-7)
**Theme**: Creation, Fall, and Early Humanity  
**Books**: Genesis 1-11  
**Key Events**: Creation, Fall, Cain & Abel, Noah's Ark, Tower of Babel  
**Chapters**: ~11 chapters  
**Focus**: God's creative work, human sin, and divine judgment/mercy

---

### **PART 2: The Patriarchs Begin** (Days 8-14)
**Theme**: Abraham's Call and Covenant  
**Books**: Genesis 12-24  
**Key Events**: Call of Abram, Covenant promises, Sodom & Gomorrah, Isaac's birth  
**Chapters**: ~13 chapters  
**Focus**: Faith, covenant, God's promises to Abraham

---

### **PART 3: Covenant Deepening** (Days 15-21)
**Theme**: Isaac, Jacob, and the Twelve Tribes  
**Books**: Genesis 25-36  
**Key Events**: Jacob & Esau, Jacob's ladder, Wrestling with God, Twelve sons  
**Chapters**: ~12 chapters  
**Focus**: God's sovereignty in family dynamics, covenant continuation

---

### **PART 4: Joseph's Journey** (Days 22-28)
**Theme**: Providence and Redemption  
**Books**: Genesis 37-50, Job (portions)  
**Key Events**: Joseph sold, Egypt, reconciliation, Jacob's blessing  
**Chapters**: ~14 chapters  
**Focus**: God's providence, suffering, redemption, forgiveness

---

## **COVERAGE SUMMARY**

**Old Testament**: ~929 chapters across 260 days (avg 3.6 chapters/day)  
**New Testament**: ~260 chapters across 105 days (avg 2.5 chapters/day)  
**Total**: 1,189 chapters across 365 days (avg 3.26 chapters/day)

**Reading Balance**:
- Narrative sections: Higher chapter count per day (flows faster)
- Poetry/Wisdom: Moderate chapter count (needs reflection)
- Law/Prophecy: Moderate chapter count (dense content)
- Epistles: Lower chapter count (theological depth)

**Chronological Approach**:
- Follows biblical timeline, not book order
- Integrates prophets with historical books
- Weaves Psalms with David's life
- Combines Gospel accounts for comprehensive view
- Reads epistles with Acts context

## **IMPLEMENTATION NOTES**

1. **Each Part** will have a folder: `part-1/`, `part-2/`, etc.
2. **Days within parts** will be numbered sequentially: Day 1-7 (Part 1), Day 8-14 (Part 2), etc.
3. **Flexibility**: Some parts may have 5-8 days depending on content density
4. **NT Integration**: New Testament passages woven throughout for theological connection
5. **Verse counts**: Will vary by genre but target 37-52 verses per day consistently

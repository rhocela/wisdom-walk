# Bible Coverage Checklist

## **PURPOSE**
Track which Bible books and chapters have been assigned to ensure:
- ✅ **Complete Coverage** - All 1,189 chapters covered exactly once
- ❌ **No Gaps** - Every chapter assigned to a study day
- ❌ **No Duplications** - No chapter appears in multiple studies

**Status Indicators:**
- ✅ = Completed and assigned to a day
- ⏳ = Pending assignment
- ❌ = Missing/needs attention
